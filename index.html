hermes

hermes is a private, single-user personal assistant that runs on its owner's own home server. It helps its owner keep track of their own email and calendar.

  • Reads the owner's Gmail messages (read-only) to summarize new mail.
  • Reads and updates the owner's Google Calendar events when the owner asks.

It is not a public service and is used only by its owner. It cannot send email.
